# Siena AI

> Siena is an empathic AI agent platform built for commerce brands. It combines AI automation with human empathy to handle customer service across email, chat, SMS, social, and WhatsApp — fully integrated with Shopify, Gorgias, Zendesk, Kustomer, and 20+ commerce tools. Used by brands like HexClad, Momentous, and True Sea Moss to automate 60–80% of support tickets.

## Product

- [Siena AI — Empathic AI Agents for Commerce](https://www.siena.cx/): AI-native customer experience platform that automates support while preserving brand voice and empathy.
- [Shopping Agent](https://www.siena.cx/shopping-agent): Turns customer conversations into conversions — answers product questions, makes recommendations, and drives revenue.
- [QA Agent](https://www.siena.cx/qa-agent): Automated conversation scoring for CX teams — measures brand voice, compliance, and resolution rate across 100% of interactions.
- [Siena Memory](https://www.siena.cx/memory): Agentic brand intelligence layer that builds customer profiles across every interaction for personalized support at scale.
- [Reviews Agent](https://www.siena.cx/ai-review-management): Automates review moderation and crafts authentic, personalized replies to scale customer trust.
- [Order Tracking](https://www.siena.cx/order-tracking): Automated order status responses integrated with Shopify and post-purchase tools — resolves WISMO tickets instantly.
- [Video](https://www.siena.cx/video): Siena watches, understands, and acts on customer videos — automates warranty claims, visual troubleshooting, and product recommendations.

## Pricing

- [Pricing](https://www.siena.cx/pricing): Conversation-based pricing model tied to ticket volume and team structure — no outcome-based fees that misalign incentives.
